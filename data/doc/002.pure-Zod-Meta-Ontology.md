### Category 1: Core Architecture & Mathematical Foundations (Primitives & Standards)

Focus: Defining fundamental numerical guarantees, branding schemas, and eliminating platform variance.

- **Integer Basis-Point Standard (`schemas/primitives.schema.ts`)**:
  - Eliminates floating-point divergence (IEEE 754 variance across AMD64, ARM64, and WebAssembly V8 isolates).
  - Defines `BasisPointsSchema = z.number().int().min(0).max(10_000)` where $10{,}000\text{ bp} \equiv 1.0$ and $1\text{ bp} \equiv 0.0001$.
  - Implements deterministic Banker's Rounding (round-half-even) integer scalar arithmetic helpers:
    $$
    \text{Let } P = A \times B, \quad Q = \lfloor P / 10{,}000 \rfloor, \quad R = P \pmod{10{,}000} \text{[cite: 2]}
    $$
    $$
    \text{Scale}(A, B) = \begin{cases} Q & \text{if } R < 5{,}000 \\ Q + 1 & \text{if } R > 5{,}000 \\ Q & \text{if } R = 5{,}000 \text{ and } Q \equiv 0 \pmod 2 \quad (\text{even}) \\ Q + 1 & \text{if } R = 5{,}000 \text{ and } Q \equiv 1 \pmod 2 \quad (\text{odd}) \end{cases} \text{[cite: 2]}
    $$
  - Enforces left-associativity for all chained calculations: $\text{Scale}(\text{Scale}(A, B), C)$.
- **Acoustic Centi-Decibel Standard (`CentiDecibelsSchema`)**:
  - Quantifies sound pressure and structural dampening as non-negative integer centi-decibels ($1\text{ dB} = 100\text{ cdB}$) to prevent rounding drift across multi-barrier topological routes:
    $$
    I_{\text{arrival}} = I_{\text{source}} - \sum_{\text{edges}} D_{\text{edge}} \text{[cite: 2]}
    $$
- **Cryptographic & Identifier Branded Primitives**:
  - `Sha256HashSchema`: Strict 64-character lowercase hexadecimal regex (`/^[0-9a-f]{64}$/`).
  - Namespaced entity identifier schemas enforcing rigid slug conventions:

    - `LocationIdSchema`: `z.string().regex(/^loc_[a-z0-9_]+$/)`
